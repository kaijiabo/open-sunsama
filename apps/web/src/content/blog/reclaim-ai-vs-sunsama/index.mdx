## How we compared them

In November 2026 we went through each app's website, pricing page, help docs, changelog and MCP docs. We looked at how each one plans a day, handles a meeting that moves, syncs calendars and works on a phone.

## Who guards your time in Reclaim vs Sunsama?

Reclaim and Sunsama both put work on your calendar. They split on who decides.

**Reclaim decides for you.** You set rules once: two hours of focus a day, lunch at noon, a gym habit three times a week. Reclaim's agents find the time and defend it.

When a meeting lands on a focus block, Reclaim moves the block. In Reclaim 2.0 it also suggests which tasks to work on in that time.

**Sunsama asks you to decide.** Each morning you pull in tasks, estimate each one and drag them onto the calendar. Sunsama adds up the hours and warns you when the day won't fit.

Since 2026 you can let Sunsama do that legwork. Its [Autoplan](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/daily-planning/) takes a short prompt, scans your connected tools and drafts the day in about 2–4 minutes. You review the draft, undo any change, and the day starts.

When a meeting runs late, you move the next blocks yourself or run the planner again. Reclaim does that part on its own.

## Where does Reclaim win?

### It defends your calendar on its own

Reclaim shines when meetings eat your week. Focus time, habits and buffer breaks rebook themselves as the calendar changes. You can also tag an event to create no-meeting days.

### It helps teams

Smart meetings find a slot that works for everyone and move when people's plans change. Scheduling links let others book you. Sunsama doesn't try to do this.

### It has a free plan

Reclaim Lite is free for one person, with a one-week scheduling range. Paid plans start at $10 a seat a month, billed yearly.

### It previews AI changes

Reclaim Chat lets you ask for changes in plain words. Changes wait in Preview Mode until you approve them. Reclaim also has an [official MCP server](https://reclaim.ai/integrations/claude) for Claude, plus a ChatGPT integration.

## Where does Sunsama win?

### It keeps you in charge

Nothing changes after you sign off on the plan. Autoplan lists every task it moved to another day, so you can keep or undo each one. People who distrust auto-schedulers like that.

### It stops you from overplanning

Sunsama totals your estimates before you commit. With planned vs actual time, you learn what a realistic day looks like. The evening shutdown helps you end on time.

### It pulls tasks from more tools

Sunsama imports tasks from Asana, ClickUp, GitHub, Gmail, Jira, Linear, Notion, Slack, Todoist, Trello and more. Since October 2026, Sunny can also search those accounts and turn what it finds into tasks. In November 2026 Sunsama added a way to connect any app that has an MCP server.

### It has phone apps and iCloud

Sunsama has iOS and Android apps, plus Mac, Windows and Linux apps. It syncs with iCloud calendars too. Reclaim [has no native phone app](https://help.reclaim.ai/en/articles/6916961-how-to-use-reclaim-on-your-mobile-device) and supports Google and Outlook only.

## Do Reclaim and Sunsama both have AI in 2026? Yes, chat and MCP

Older posts say Reclaim has AI and Sunsama doesn't. That is out of date.

- **Reclaim's AI runs the calendar.** Its agents place focus time, habits and breaks. Reclaim Chat, its MCP server and its ChatGPT app all stage changes in a preview first.
- **Sunsama's AI helps you plan.** Autoplan drafts the day, and Sunny can create and move tasks, review your schedule and clean up your backlog. Sunny and the MCP server [left beta in August 2026](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/changelog/weekly-product-changelog-august-28-2026).

Neither offers a documented public REST API. Sunsama's [API request](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/improvements/p/sunsama-api) has been open since December 2019, with 888 votes. We found no public API docs for Reclaim either.

If you want to script your planner or run it on your own server, that is where Open Sunsama differs. It has a public REST API, and its code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/kaijiabo/open-sunsama).

## What does a busy Tuesday look like in each app?

Here is a busy Tuesday, based on how each app describes its flow. It is an example, not a measurement: five tasks, four meetings, and one new meeting added at 10am.

**In Reclaim,** your two focus hours and lunch are already on the calendar. The new 10am meeting lands on your focus block. Reclaim moves the block to the afternoon without you doing anything.

**In Sunsama,** Autoplan drafted the day at 9am and you approved it in a few minutes. The new meeting overlaps a task block. You drag that task to 3pm and push a smaller one to Wednesday.

Both days can end with the same work done. The Reclaim day takes less effort. The Sunsama day leaves you with a clearer sense of what you chose.
