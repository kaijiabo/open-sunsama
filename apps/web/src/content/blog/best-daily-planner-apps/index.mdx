## How did we compare these daily planner apps?

In November 2026 we checked each app's website, pricing page, help docs, App Store page, and MCP or API docs. We looked at five things:

- **Can you see if today fits?** Tasks and meetings in one view, with time estimates.
- **Can you time block easily?** Drag a task onto the calendar, then move it when plans change.
- **What happens to unfinished tasks?** Rollover, so you don't rebuild the list each morning.
- **Does it work where you work?** Calendars, platforms and phones.
- **Can an AI help?** An MCP server or API for Claude, ChatGPT and other agents.

We also read user threads on Reddit, Hacker News and Product Hunt. We did not run long trials of every app. We build and use Open Sunsama every day.

## 2. Structured: best visual timeline on a phone

**Verdict:** the clearest way to see your day on a phone. Tasks and events sit on one vertical timeline.

**Best for:** iPhone users and visual thinkers who plan on the go.

**What it does well:** the timeline is easy to read at a glance. Pro adds Structured AI to draft your day, Replan for missed tasks, recurring routines and calendar import.

The timeline, inbox and tasks are free. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, Android and the web, though the Android and web apps trail the iPhone app.

**Where it falls short:** calendar import from Google, Outlook or iCloud needs Pro, and it is one-way into Structured. There is no Windows or Linux app, and we found no API or MCP server.

**Pick it if** you plan mostly on your phone and want a simple timeline. See our [Structured vs Sunsama](/blog/structured-vs-sunsama) comparison.

## 3. Sunsama: best guided daily ritual

**Verdict:** the calmest paid planner. A short morning plan helps you commit to a day that fits.

**Best for:** people who overplan and want a ritual to start and end each day.

**What it does well:** Sunsama totals your estimates before you commit. It imports tasks from Asana, Jira, Linear, Notion, Todoist, Trello, Gmail and more.

Its new [Autoplan](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/daily-planning/) agent, in beta and on for new workspaces, drafts your day from a short prompt in about 2–4 minutes. You review and undo anything before the day starts.

Sunny, its AI assistant, and its official MCP server both left beta in August 2026. Autoplan, a beta AI agent, drafts your day for you to review. It has apps for every platform.

**Where it falls short:** there is no public REST API and no free plan, only a 14-day trial. Some users say the daily ritual became [a 20-minute chore](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProductivityApps/comments/1utkevs/people_who_pay_monthly_for_a_planner_app_motion/oxnyccj/).

**Pick it if** you want guided planning and lots of task imports. Read our [Sunsama review](/blog/sunsama-review).

## How do you plan your day in a daily planner app?

Whichever app you pick, the same five steps give you a calm, realistic day:

1. **Brain dump.** Put every task in the backlog.
2. **Pick three to five tasks** for today.
3. **Estimate each one.** If the total is more than your free time, move something to tomorrow.
4. **Block time** on the calendar between meetings.
5. **Shut down.** Check off what's done and let the rest roll over.
